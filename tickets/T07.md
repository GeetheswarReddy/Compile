# Ticket T07: Generation Step Functions Flow

## Context Digest

Generation uses Bedrock Converse, verifies each candidate before serving, retries immediately at most three times with two-second candidate budgets and an eight-second overall timeout. Success stores a separate learner/topic Prepared Question; stale output is discarded, while three failures increment a Topic failure counter and serve closest Curated Seeded Question.
